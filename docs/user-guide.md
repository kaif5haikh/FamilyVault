# Use FamilyVault

## Open your vault

Choose **Family access**, then **Create a local vault** to start on one phone.
Set a device screen lock first. Enter the vault name and your display name.
Use **Unlock vault** when prompted. The app locks in the background, after
five minutes of inactivity or when you choose **Lock vault** in Settings.

For a connected vault, each family member signs in with their own verified
account. Request approval for each new phone. The administrator confirms
the displayed device fingerprint and manages invitations and access.
Approved family members can read and edit shared records and documents.
[Family access setup](supabase-development.md#provision-a-development-family).

## Record family money

- Add cash and bank accounts, then record income, expenses and transfers from
  **Transactions**. Set budgets and reconcile statement balances under the
  account and planning screens.
- Use **Assets** for properties, land, metals and investments. Record the
  purchase basis, family share and valuation dates. Update valuations as new
  information becomes available. The app keeps the earlier valuations.
- Use **Loans & lending** for borrowed and lent principal, interest terms and
  actual payments. Split a payment into principal and interest using the
  values you recorded.
- Use **Business** for income, costs, stock movements and worker payments.
  Choose a date range to see profit, stock cost and salaries.
- Correct an entry from its detail page. FamilyVault keeps the original and
  posts the reversal or corrected replacement. Use archive and restore when
  an account or record permits that change.

## Add documents

Open **Documents** to import a PDF or image or scan with the camera. Give it
a title and category. Choose an owner and add an expiry date if needed. You
can open an older version, link a document to an existing transaction or
holding, archive it and restore it later. A newly approved phone may need
another enrolled device online to receive keys for older documents.

Use **Document expiry notifications** in Settings to opt in on a phone.
Alerts use a generic message. Exact dates and document details appear inside
the unlocked vault. When you export a decrypted document to Files, that copy
is managed by the selected file provider.

## Sync after working offline

Create and edit records while an authorized vault is open, then reconnect
and choose **Check sync** in Settings if needed. Changes stay on the device
until the server confirms them. If someone else changed the same revision,
use **Saved device changes** to review the conflict. Recovery keeps the
previous encrypted cache and its pending records so you can review them.
The local-only vault has its own records and backup flow.

## Back up and restore

Open **Encrypted backups** from Settings. Create a backup, save a verified
copy in Files and keep the recovery key separately. You may connect Google
Drive, verify the displayed account and save an independent copy there.
Daily or weekly schedules run when the vault is open.

On a clean installation, select **Restore an encrypted backup** and open
the Files copy or choose a Drive version. Enter its recovery key, verify it,
then restore the local vault. A recovered copy keeps its recorded history.
Sign in and enroll independently to open the current shared workspace.
[Full recovery guide](backup-recovery.md).

# Use FamilyVault

## Get started

Open FamilyVault and choose **Take a short tour** or **Start using FamilyVault**.
Choose **Set up on this phone** to start your own records. Set a phone screen
lock first. Enter the family name and the name you want people to see on changes.
Use **Unlock records** when prompted. The app locks in the background, after
five idle minutes or when you choose **Lock FamilyVault** in Settings.

Use **Sign in to a family** for shared records. Each person uses their own
account. Show the approval code on a new phone to the family administrator.
They can compare it and approve the phone. Approved family members can read
and edit shared records and documents. Use the **Family** tab to see who
has access.
[Family access setup](supabase-development.md#provision-a-development-family).

## Record family money

- Add a bank or cash account under **Money → Accounts**. From Home, choose
  **Add money received**, **Add money spent** or **Move money**. Enter the
  amount, choose the account and check the date.
- Under **Money → Spending plans**, set a monthly amount for the things
  you plan to spend on. Open an account to compare it with a bank statement.
- Use **What we own** for property, gold, vehicles and investments. Enter your
  family's share and update the estimated value when you have a new one.
  Earlier estimates stay in the item's history.
- Use **What we owe** for money the family borrowed or lent. Add payments
  from the loan's details. Record the amounts paid toward the loan and in
  interest using your payment statement.
- Use **More → Business** for sales, costs, stock and wages. Choose a month
  to see its profit and how it was worked out.
- Open **Money activity** to find and correct a record. FamilyVault keeps the
  original and records what changed.

## Add documents

Open **Documents → Add a document** to choose a PDF or image or use the
camera. Give it a name and category, say whose document it is and add an
expiry date if needed. You can search by name, open the document or an older
version, link it to an item or money record, or archive it. A newly approved
phone may need another approved phone online to open older family documents.

Use **Document expiry notifications** in Settings to opt in on a phone.
Alerts use a generic message. Exact dates and document details appear inside
the unlocked vault. When you export a decrypted document to Files, that copy
is managed by the selected file provider.

## Update your family after working offline

While your family records are open, you can save changes without internet.
Reconnect and choose **Check for updates** in Settings if needed. The phone
keeps changes until it gets confirmation. If two people changed the same
record, use **Changes saved on this phone** to review the differences.

## Make and use a safe copy

Open **More → Safe copies**. Make a safe copy, save the file in Files and keep
the recovery key separately. If Google Drive is set up, check the account
shown before saving another copy there. Daily or weekly schedules run while
your records are open.

On a phone with no FamilyVault records, choose **Bring back saved data**
during setup. Choose the file or a Drive copy, enter its recovery key, and
choose **Check this copy**. When the check passes, choose **Bring back my
records**. The copy keeps its history. To open your family's current shared
records, sign in and have the phone approved.
[Full recovery guide](backup-recovery.md).

## Get help

Open **Show me how** from More. Search for a task such as gold, bank, loan
or safe copy. The guide gives a short explanation and opens the screen
where you can try it. You can read the guide without internet. You can also
take the tour again from More.

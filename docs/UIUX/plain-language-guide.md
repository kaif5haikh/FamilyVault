# Words to use in FamilyVault

Use the words a family member would use when asking about money or papers.
Use a short label for the action and put essential qualifications next to it.

| Task | Words people see | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See a recorded cash and bank total | Money available | Adds up the cash and bank amounts recorded in the app. |
| Record new money | Add money received | Records money such as salary, rent or a payment. |
| Record a payment | Add money spent | Records money leaving the account for a purchase or bill. |
| Transfer between accounts | Move money | Moves an amount between two recorded accounts. |
| Browse journals | Money activity | Shows the name, date, amount, type and account for a record. |
| Reconcile an account | Check that the amounts match | Compares a statement with the app on a chosen day. |
| View holdings | What we own | Shows items such as gold, property, vehicles and investments. |
| View borrowing and lending | What we owe | Shows remaining loan amounts. Money lent to others has its own amount. |
| View net worth | Estimated family value | Uses what the family has recorded as owned, minus what it owes. |
| Record a valuation | Update estimated value | Uses a dated estimate for the item and the family's recorded share. |
| Correct a posting | Correct this record or Undo this record | Keeps the original and adds the correction or undo entry. |
| Wait for a shared update | Saved on this phone. Waiting to update the family. | The local write finished. Sharing still needs confirmation. |
| Back up records | Safe copies | A protected file and a separately stored recovery key. |
| Restore a backup | Bring back saved data | Checks the file and key before bringing records onto a phone. |

Display the source of a record and its status using words. Keep exact
financial information such as interest, the family share, recovery keys and
phone approval codes when the person needs it to act safely. Explain these
where they are used. Leave accounting lines and change history available
in details.

English (India) is the default. The language picker offers Hindi in
Devanagari on the first screen and in Settings. Use everyday words such
as «पैसे मिले», «पैसे खर्च हुए», «हमें कितना देना है» and «दस्तावेज़ जोड़ें».
Use familiar words like «हिसाब», «रकम», «कर्ज़» and «इजाज़त».

Use `VaultStrings` for shared labels and help. Use named placeholders
when a message includes an amount, date or name. Keep exact Indian
rupee formatting and use «लाख» and «करोड़» in Hindi summaries. Names
and notes entered by the family keep their original wording.

The existing Marathi catalogs are preserved. Phones with an older
Marathi preference use English until the user chooses English or Hindi.

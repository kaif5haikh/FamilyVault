# Finding your way around

| Tab | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Check money available, see this month's received and spent amounts, add a record or document and review recent activity. |
| Money | Open Accounts, Money activity, What we own, What we owe, Spending plans and Reports. |
| Documents | Add a document, search by name, narrow by category and open a saved version. |
| Family | See people with access. An administrator with a connected family can verify access, invite members and manage phones. |
| More | Open Safe copies, Business, Settings, Show me how or the tour. |

One indexed navigation shell holds the five tabs and keeps a previously
opened tab's search and position during a session. Section lists, editors and
detail pages open above the tabs. Back returns to the page the person left.
An independently opened section has a Back button to Money or More. App lock
removes the workspace and still requires device verification.

Home shows one money summary, a group of daily actions and at most three
recent records. The estimated family value is expandable when applicable.
Reports holds the charts and period totals. The controls stay large even
when the screen needs to scroll at high text sizes.

The first welcome page offers **Start using FamilyVault**, **Take a short
tour**, **Show me how** and a language picker for English and Hindi. The
tour has four steps for money, documents,
family sign-in and safe copies. The person can go back, skip or finish and
then choose how to open their records. Finishing, skipping or starting
directly remembers that choice on the phone. Existing protected records
continue to open through device verification. **More → Take the tour
again** opens it manually.

The tour flag and language choice use existing device-only secure settings.
Use **More → Settings → Language** to switch at any time.
These settings do not contain family records or access credentials.
